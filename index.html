Bio

Atharva Aalok is a founding engineer at Godela and a Ph.D. student at MIT, building methods that accelerate the design of mechanical and aerospace products: agentic CAD, high-fidelity and machine-learned simulation, and learned design optimization. Atharva holds an M.S. in Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering from Stanford University, earned in the Aerospace Design Lab, and a B.Tech in Aerospace Engineering from IIT Madras, finishing first in the department.

Selected research

NIGnets: Neural Injective Geometry Networks

A neural architecture with a hard guarantee: every shape it represents is a simple closed curve or surface. Meshes stay watertight, and optimizers search only over physically valid designs.

code · paper · docs · talk

Robust shape optimization with neural shape representations and neural operators

Neural-operator surrogates (FNOGNO, GINO, BNO) trained on SU2 Euler and RANS data predict pressure and skin friction to within 2%, while NIGnet and NeuralODE geometry keeps the optimizer on valid, in-distribution shapes.

With Jiayi Zhou (co-first author), Valentin Duruisseaux, Xinyi Li, Juan J. Alonso and Anima Anandkumar · code coming soon

Aerodynamic shape optimization with deep reinforcement learning

PPO and actor-critic agents reshape NIGnet airfoils to maximize lift-to-drag in XFoil, reaching L/D of 61.64. Hindsight Experience Replay lets a single policy design to any target L/D.

code · paper · poster
