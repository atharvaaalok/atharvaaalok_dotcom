Selected research

NIGnets: Neural Injective Geometry Networks

Neural networks that can't draw a self-intersecting shape. The guarantee is built into the architecture. Monotonic networks replace activation functions to add expressivity without breaking it, and pre-auxiliary networks turn NIGnets into generative models over a latent space of valid shapes.

2024 – 2025 · code · paper · docs · talk

Robust shape optimization with neural shape representations and neural operators

Fast surrogates, valid shapes. Neural operators (FNOGNO, GINO, BNO) trained on ~20,000 Euler and ~20,000 RANS simulations predict pressure and skin friction within 2%. A NeuralODE shape model that cannot self-intersect reconstructs every shape in the dataset to 10−8 L2 error, so the optimizer stays on valid, in-distribution designs.

2024 – 2026 · with Jiayi Zhou (co-first author), Valentin Duruisseaux, Xinyi Li, Juan J. Alonso and Anima Anandkumar · code coming soon

Aerodynamic shape optimization with deep reinforcement learning

An RL agent as the airfoil designer. PPO and advantage actor-critic agents reshape airfoils in XFoil to an L/D of 61.64. NIGnet and NICE geometry keep the search on valid shapes, and Hindsight Experience Replay lets one policy hit any target L/D.

2025 · code · paper · poster

Short bio

Atharva Aalok is a founding engineer at Godela (YC X25), building agentic CAD for mechanical and aerospace design, and is on leave from the Mechanical Engineering Ph.D. program at MIT, advised by Faez Ahmed. Atharva holds an M.S. in Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering from Stanford, earned in Juan J. Alonso's Aerospace Design Lab, and a B.Tech in Aerospace Engineering from IIT Madras, graduating first in the department. Atharva's research spans neural geometry representations, neural-operator surrogates and reinforcement learning for shape optimization.
